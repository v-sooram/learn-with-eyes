    Class 5 • Unit I — Living Things

    🌿 Growing Plants

    🌱
    Why Are Plants Important?
    Plants are living things that help us every day

    Plants are living things. They play a very important role in our lives.

    🍚
    Food
    Rice, wheat, fruits, vegetables
    🍬
    Sugar & Spices
    Sugar, tea, coffee, spices
    🪵
    Wood & Fibre
    Wood, rubber, cotton
    💨
    Oxygen
    For us to breathe
    🫘
    How Do Plants Grow? — From Seeds!
    Reproduction by Seeds

    We can grow plants through seeds. Plants grow from seeds into seedlings. The seedlings grow into big plants with stems, roots, and leaves.

    💡 Germination = The process by which a seed grows into a new plant or seedling.
    💧
    Water
    ☀️
    Sunlight
    🌬️
    Air
    🌡️
    Warmth
    💨
    How Seeds Travel — Seed Dispersal
    Seeds need to spread away from the parent plant

    Nature helps by scattering seeds away. This is called seed dispersal.

    🌬️
    By Wind
    Dandelion, maple, drumstick
    🌊
    By Water
    Coconut, lotus
    🐦
    By Animals
    Mango, cherry, cocklebur
    💥
    By Bursting
    Balsam, pea, okra
    🪴
    Growing Plants Without Seeds!
    Vegetative Reproduction

    Some plants grow from stems, roots or leaves — not from seeds! This is called vegetative reproduction.

    🌹
    From Stems
    Rose, hibiscus, money plant
    🥔
    From Roots
    Sweet potato, carrot, radish
    🍃
    From Leaves
    Bryophyllum
    👨‍🌾
    Agriculture — Growing Crops
    The science of growing plants on a large scale

    The practice of growing plants on a large scale for food is called agriculture.

    ☀️
    Kharif Crops
    Summer — rice, jowar, bajra
    ❄️
    Rabi Crops
    Winter — wheat, gram, peas

    Class 4 • Unit I — Food and Health

    🍎 Food – Our Basic Need

    🍽️
    Why Do We Need Food?
    Food gives us energy, helps us grow, and protects us from sickness

    Food is our basic need. It helps us:

    ⚡
    Get Energy
    To play, run and study
    💪
    Grow Strong
    To build our body
    🛡️
    Fight Sickness
    To stay healthy
    🔧
    Repair Body
    To fix damaged parts
    🥗
    Nutrients in Our Food
    Tiny parts in food that keep us healthy
    ⚡
    Carbohydrates
    Energy-Giving
    Rice, roti, potato, sugar
    🧈
    Fats
    Stored Energy + Warmth
    Ghee, oil, butter, nuts
    💪
    Proteins
    Body-Building
    Dal, eggs, milk, paneer
    🛡️
    Vitamins & Minerals
    Protective
    Fruits, vegetables, milk
    💧
    Water
    Keeps our body hydrated
    🧹
    Roughage / Fibre
    Cleans our stomach
    ⚖️
    Balanced Diet
    Eating the right amount of all nutrients every day

    A balanced diet contains the right amount of all nutrients. It keeps us healthy and full of energy.

    💡 Eat a colourful plate: brown (rice/roti), white (milk/curd), green (vegetables), red/orange (fruits) — every day!
    🧼
    Good Eating Habits
    How to eat healthy and stay safe
    🧼
    Wash Hands
    Before every meal
    ⏱️
    Eat on Time
    Fixed meal times
    🤐
    Chew Well
    Mouth closed
    🚫
    Avoid Street Food
    Uncovered food
    🥫
    Food Preservation
    Ways to save food so it doesn't spoil
    🧊
    Refrigeration
    Milk, vegetables stay cold
    ☀️
    Sun Drying
    Removes water — chillies, fish
    🧂
    Pickling
    Salt + oil — mangoes, lemons
    🥫
    Canning
    Heating + sealing jars

    🦷 Teeth, Microbes & Digestion

    🦷
    Two Sets of Teeth
    Milk teeth and permanent teeth
    👶
    Milk / Temporary Teeth
    20 teeth
    Start appearing around 6 months old
    🧍
    Permanent Teeth
    32 teeth
    Replaces milk teeth as we grow
    🔬
    Structure of a Tooth
    Crown, root, enamel, dentine and pulp
    👑
    Crown
    Visible top part
    🌳
    Root
    Hidden part inside gums
    💎
    Enamel
    Hardest white outer shell
    🟡
    Dentine
    Yellow middle layer
    🩸
    Pulp
    Soft inner core — nerves & blood
    ✂️
    Kinds of Teeth
    Different teeth do different jobs
    ✂️
    Incisors (8)
    Flat front — Biting & cutting
    🗡️
    Canines (4)
    Pointy side — Tearing food
    🔨
    Premolars (8)
    Broad middle — Crushing & cracking
    ⚙️
    Molars (12)
    Large flat back — Grinding & chewing
    🪥
    Tooth Decay & Care
    How cavities form — and how to prevent them
    ⚠️ Cavity chain: Sweet food → Sticky germs (plaque) → Acid forms → Hole (cavity)
    🪥
    Brush Twice
    Up and down motion
    🧵
    Floss
    Between teeth
    👅
    Clean Tongue
    Every morning
    🍎
    Eat Fruits
    Instead of sticky candies
    🦠
    Microbes — Tiny Living Things
    Some are harmful, some are helpful!
    🦠
    Bacteria
    Typhoid ⚠️ / Curd from milk ✅
    🍄
    Fungi
    Ringworm ⚠️ / Soft bread with yeast ✅
    🤧
    Virus
    Flu, cold, polio ⚠️
    🦟
    Protozoa
    Malaria ⚠️
    🍎➡️⚡
    Digestion — From Food to Energy
    The journey of food through our body
    👄
    1. Mouth
    Teeth chew + saliva mixes
    🥖
    2. Food Pipe
    Pushes food to stomach
    🫄
    3. Stomach
    Juices churn food into paste
    🌀
    4. Small Intestine
    Nutrients absorbed into blood
    🍩
    5. Large Intestine
    Soaks up extra water
    🚽
    6. Anus
    Removes undigested waste
