## 2. Structure of a Tooth & Kinds of Teeth

### Visual & ISL Video Resources
- **ISL Video Resource:** Learn the anatomical structure using the **Class 4 Structure of Tooth Overview**.
- **Interactive Web Diagram (Tooth Anatomy):**
  - **Crown (Outer View):** Show the visible top part.
  - **Root (Inner View):** Show the bottom embedded inside the gums.
  - **Layers:** Color-code **Enamel** (hard white shell), **Dentine** (yellow middle layer), and **Pulp** (soft inner core with pink blood vessels and nerves).
- **Kinds of Teeth Visual Grid:**
  - ✂️ **Incisors (8 total):** Flat front teeth → Biting & cutting.
  - 🗡️ **Canines (4 total):** Pointy side teeth → Tearing food.
  - 🔨 **Premolars (8 total):** Broad middle teeth → Crushing & cracking.
  - ⚙️ **Molars (12 total):** Large flat back teeth → Grinding & chewing.

> **Note:** For a quick reference on how to structure the tooth anatomy part visually, see the **Class 4 Structure of Tooth Demonstration**, which clearly breaks down the crown, root, enamel, and pulp into basic parts that can easily be adapted into simplified visual diagrams or sign-supported visual cards.

### Illustrated Notations (Page Footer)
- **Enamel:** [Icon: Diamond 💎 + Tooth] → Hardest outer white part of the tooth.
- **Pulp:** [Icon: Heartbeat / Blood Vessel 🩸] → Soft center inside the tooth containing nerves.
- **Gums:** [Icon: Pink frame holding tooth] → Pink tissue holding the teeth tightly in place.

## 3. Tooth Decay & Taking Care of Teeth

### Visual & ISL Video Resources
- **Visual Format:** Animated Before vs. After loop sequence showing cavity formation.
  - Sweet food → Sticky germs (Plaque) → Acid forms → Hole (Cavity).
- **Looping 2-Second GIFs (Care Steps):**
  - 🪥 Brushing twice daily (up and down motion).
  - 🧵 Flossing between teeth.
  - 👅 Cleaning the tongue.
  - 🍎 Eating fresh fruits instead of sticky candies.

### Illustrated Notations (Page Footer)
- **Plaque:** [Icon: Sticky yellow layer 🟡 + Germs] → A sticky layer of germs that forms on dirty teeth.
- **Cavity:** [Icon: Tooth with a black hole 🕳️] → A hole caused by tooth decay.

## 5. Digestion & Process of Digestion

### Visual & ISL Video Resources
- **Visual Format:** Interactive Alimentary Canal Journey (Flowchart Animation).
  1. 👄 **Mouth:** Teeth chew + Saliva mixes.
  2. 🥖 **Food Pipe (Oesophagus):** Pushes food down to stomach.
  3. 🫄 **Stomach:** Juices churn food into liquid paste.
  4. 🌀 **Small Intestine:** Nutrients absorbed into the blood.
  5. 🍩 **Large Intestine:** Soaks up extra water.
  6. 🚽 **Anus:** Removes undigested waste out of the body.

### Illustrated Notations (Page Footer)
- **Digestion:** [Icon: Apple 🍎 → Energy ⚡] → Breaking down food into tiny parts so the body can use it.
- **Saliva:** [Icon: Water droplet in mouth 💧] → Liquid in the mouth that softens food.
